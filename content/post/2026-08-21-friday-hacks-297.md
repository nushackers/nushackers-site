## 1) Evaluating AI Agents @ Airwallex

How do you know an agent is genuinely ready to ship?

This sharing details the evaluation loop behind Kai, Airwallex's AI agent.

James Li is an Engineering Manager in AI at Airwallex, leading the team behind Kai. He is also joined by Weikun and Hao-Tong, AI engineers at Airwallex who work on AI Agents and LLM Modeling respectively.

## 2) LiquidJS - Building and Growing Open Source

What happens when a small open-source project becomes software that thousands depend on? This talk explores how a template engine works and what growing open source actually looks like.

#### Speaker Profile 🎙️

Jun Yang has worked as a software engineer and architect at Microsoft, TikTok, and other tech companies. He is the creator of LiquidJS, a widely used JavaScript template engine.
