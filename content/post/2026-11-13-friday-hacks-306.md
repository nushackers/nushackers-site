## 1) C++ as an Interpreted Language

Many would tell you that C++ is a compiled language, but did you know that it can be interpreted as well? In this talk, we explore the growing compile-time support of C++.

#### Speaker Profile 🎙️

James Lim has been a C++ hobbyist for the last year and actively explores corners of the standard. He spoke at CppCon this year on how to utilise C++26 reflections and is happy to talk about C++ anytime 🙂

<br /><br />

## 2) Applied Research in the Age of AI

How do we integrate AI, with its infinite wisdom and infinite sycophancy, as part of research involving real-world problems that may not have verifiable proofs?

#### Speaker Profile 🎙️

Devansh Shah graduated from the NUS School of Computing in 2025 and was part of the NUS Hackers Coreteam as an undergraduate. He currently works as a quantitative trader-researcher at Optiver, where he develops systematic trading strategies.
