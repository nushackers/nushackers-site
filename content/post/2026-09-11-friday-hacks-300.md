## 1) The Long Tail of AI: Why the Future Isn't Just One Big Super-Model

Join us as we talk about which open-source models are actually being used, why "one giant model fits all" is dying in favor of multi-model pipelines and specialized micro-models and the real-world use cases of such scenarios.

#### Speaker Profile 🎙️

Ron Tay is a Business Lead at Featherless AI and a fintech founder. He has spent the past year leading Featherless AI's Singapore inference team and business development in Asia.

<br /><br />

## 2) Ten Years of Maintaining a Small Open Source Project: Lessons from GoDNS

Most open source projects never go viral. GoDNS, a small dynamic-DNS tool I have maintained since 2014, quietly grew to 1.8k stars, 21+ DNS providers and a steady stream of strangers' pull requests. This talk covers the unglamorous side of open source: what changes once users show up, how to say no to feature requests without making enemies, how to get strangers to contribute, and how to keep maintaining for a decade without burning out.

#### Speaker Profile 🎙️

Timothy Ye is a Senior Software Engineer at JPMorgan Chase with more than 14 years of experience building backend systems, data pipelines, monitoring platforms, and infrastructure automation across finance and e-commerce, including previously at Shopee. He maintains more than 40 open source repositories with over 4.8k GitHub stars, including GoDNS and skm, and is an NUS Master of Computing alumnus.
